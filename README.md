# Bounded Power Framework

A framework for describing authority by what it does, and for checking each of its powers against a short set of structural limits: power stays small enough to stop, harm stays interruptible, and mistakes stay correctable.
<br clear="all">

> **v1, published for independent review.** No institution or government has adopted it. The rules are a proposal, published so that others can test and criticize them.

Power tends to grow when it is left unattended: it widens, outlasts its purpose, and becomes difficult to question or reverse. The framework answers with one inversion: *authority does not continue by default.* Each power must justify its continuation at set intervals. A power that still meets the requirements is renewed and continues; one that cannot be justified ends, and the institution carries on without it.

## Three ways in

1. **[Something happened to me](00-Reading/00-Start-Here.md#1-something-happened-to-me):** putting on record what an institution did.
2. **[Examine an institution](00-Reading/00-Start-Here.md#2-examine-an-institution):** recording how its powers are built and what can stop them.
3. **[Understand or evaluate the framework](00-Reading/00-Start-Here.md#3-understand-or-evaluate-the-framework):** the reasoning and the rules.

[Start Here](00-Reading/00-Start-Here.md) explains each path.

## What is in v1

- **The Core Requirements:** 101 numbered rules in seven parts, including four **Floors**, minimum protections beneath which no authority may push a person. They are the framework's only binding text: [SCBP-04](01-Framework/SCBP-04-Core-Requirements.md). The framework documents, SCBP-01 to SCBP-09, form the *Structural Constitution of Bounded Power*.
- **Forms and tools:** a registration form that records an institution one power at a time, a witness report form, and an authority response form, with a shared vocabulary, a checker, and two AI bundles that help people complete them: [02-Forms](02-Forms/).
- **Worked examples:** 32 fictional institutions recorded with the forms, from police departments and water utilities to data brokers and libraries, with witness reports and institutions' responses: [03-Examples](03-Examples/).
- **BPF's own record:** the project recorded against its own rules: [04-Registry](04-Registry/).

v1 holds no records of real institutions. A record or a report that anyone makes with the forms belongs to its author, who decides whether to publish it ([Terms](TERMS.md)). The framework is designed for a later stage: a [public register](04-Registry/README.md#the-public-register-planned) of records, with aggregate reports across them, intended to open only after review and under published operating rules.

## Who makes this

BPF is maintained by one volunteer, publishing under the GitHub account **WitnessMark**. AI assistants (Claude, by Anthropic) help draft, check and edit much of the text, the forms and the checker; the maintainer decides what is published. The project's [own record](04-Registry/SCBP-REG-0001-BPF.md) describes its powers and gaps against its own rules.

## Guiding principle

**Prevent harm where possible. Repair harm where necessary.**

## Questions and critical review

The framework is published for examination, criticism and improvement. Questions, objections, corrections and proposed revisions are welcome in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/WitnessMark/Bounded-Power-Framework/discussions): about the instructions, errors or gaps in the documents, the framework's assumptions, possible applications, and improvements.

*Discussions is not a place for witness reports, identifying details, private documents or sensitive evidence.* v1 accepts no records or reports. GitHub Issues stay closed while the review process is developed.

The project's public contact is the GitHub account **WitnessMark** and the address **WitnessMarkGitHub@proton.me**, for comments on the framework itself, not for personal information or reports about real people or institutions.

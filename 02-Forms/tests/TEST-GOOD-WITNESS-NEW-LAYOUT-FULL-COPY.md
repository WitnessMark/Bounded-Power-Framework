## 1. What happened *(in your own words)*

> I was waiting for the 7 bus at the stop on Delancey and 5th, right outside the Suds laundromat, around 6:40 at night. It was still light out. A guy on a bike rode past on the sidewalk and a police car pulled up hard in front of him, half on the curb. Two officers got out. I want to be clear about that, his hands were up. Dorsey put him on the ground face down and put his knee on his back. The guy said "I can't breathe, you're on my back" maybe three or four times. I said I have a right to film and I'm not in the way. He didn't take it.
>

Another car came and the officers talked for a while and then the bike was put in the trunk and they left. I found out later from the news that his name is Marcus Hale and he was charged with riding on the sidewalk and resisting, and the resisting charge got dropped.

## 1. Statement

On October 14, 2026, at about 6:40 p.m., at the bus stop on Delancey Street and 5th Street, outside the Suds laundromat, Riverton, I, Sam Okafor, saw two officers of the Riverton Police Department pull a man off his bike and hold him face down on the ground.

I was waiting for the 7 bus, about 15 feet away. It was still light. Nothing was in my way.

A man was riding a bike on the sidewalk. A police car pulled up hard in front of him, half on the curb. Two officers got out. The driver was a tall white man. His nameplate said DORSEY and his badge number was 4471. I read them when he walked past me, and I am sure of them. The second officer was a shorter woman. I could not see her name.

Officer Dorsey grabbed the man by his backpack and pulled him off the bike. The man said "what did I do, what did I do". His hands were up the whole time.

Officer Dorsey put the man face down on the ground and put his knee on the man's back. The second officer held his legs. The man said "I can't breathe, you're on my back" three or four times.

I started filming. Rosa, who works in the laundromat, came out and shouted at the officers to get off him. Officer Dorsey looked at us and said "back up or you're going in the car too". I stepped back and kept filming. He said "turn that off", then "delete that or I'll take your phone as evidence". I said I had a right to film and was not in the way. He did not take my phone.

The knee stayed on his back for about two minutes. Then the officers sat the man up. He was coughing badly. They put him in the police car. Nobody told us why he was stopped. A second police car came. The bike went into a trunk, and the officers left. About four other people at the bus stop saw it.

The man was hurt: he was coughing badly when they sat him up. I was threatened with arrest and told to delete my video. I have not used that bus stop since.

On October 15, I called the department's non-emergency line, 555-0142, at about 9:15 a.m. I was told to use the online form. The same day I filed a complaint with the Riverton Office of Police Accountability, case OPA-2026-0912. On October 29 they emailed that it is "under review". Later I read in the news that the man is Marcus Hale, that he was charged with riding on the sidewalk and resisting, and that the resisting charge was dropped.

Before writing this, I posted my own video. I have not watched anyone else's video of it.

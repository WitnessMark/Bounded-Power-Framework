Bounded Power Framework

Keeping power small enough to stop.

Institutions make decisions that bind people's lives. Left unattended, their powers tend to widen, outlast their purpose, and become difficult to question or reverse. That is an ordinary tendency, not a moral failure, and it can be answered with structure.

The framework rests on one idea: authority does not continue by default. Each power has to show, at set intervals, that it is still justified. A power that is renewed carries on. One that cannot be justified ends, and the institution carries on without it.

v1, published for independent review. No institution or government has adopted it.

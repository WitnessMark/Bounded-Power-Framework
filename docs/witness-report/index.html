Make a witness report

This page is open inside another app. Reports often cannot be saved from inside apps. Open it in the phone's own browser first: tap the menu (often ⋯ or ⋮) and choose Open in browser, Open in Safari or Open in Chrome.

A signed statement of something an organization or an office did, failed to do, or threatened to do, with your own words kept beside it. Written like a police witness statement: when and where, who, and what happened, in order.

If anyone is in immediate danger, emergency services come first. A report is a record, not a rescue.
  • About fifteen minutes. Anything not known can be left blank.
  • Nothing is sent or stored. The page works only inside this browser. At the end, the report is saved to the phone or computer, or sent wherever its author chooses.
  • Names and details are kept. The report is a record to help you remember and to identify what happened: names, badge numbers, car numbers, descriptions, places. At the end you can also make a copy for sharing that identifies no private person.

A registration record

A registration record is a separate document describing an organization's powers. Most reports have none.

Map coordinates, and the file name

Coordinates only from a map app, never estimated, in decimal degrees.

File fingerprints

A code calculated from a file. A matching code later shows the file has not changed; it does not show when the file existed. The file is read only inside this browser. If the organization holds the same file, such as a letter it sent, it can match the code and learn who wrote the report.

A verification phrase

Lets a person who used a made-up name or no name step forward later. At least five random words, nothing personal. Only a code calculated from it goes in the report. Written down and kept safe first: the phrase is saved nowhere and cannot be recovered from the code.

Your statement

This statement is put together from your answers. It holds nothing you did not say. Read it, and change anything that is wrong or reads badly. Your own words stay as you wrote them, beside it.

Check and save

Which copy?

Replace each detail that points to a private person with a description in square brackets, such as [a bystander] or [the man in handcuffs]: your own name, bystanders, the person it was done to, private addresses, licence plates of private cars, phone numbers, account numbers. Officers and officials on duty keep their names and numbers.

How much of the place should show?

Save or send opens the phone's own menu: save to Files, email it to yourself, or send it. An email to yourself also shows when it was written.


  
After saving

  
The report belongs to its author. Nothing has been sent anywhere, and this project has no place to file reports. The full report names people. Sharing or publishing it can expose you to retaliation or a legal claim, so think about who receives it. A lawyer, a legal aid service or an oversight body can act on it. Before sending the copy for sharing, read it once as a stranger would: this page cannot find every name.

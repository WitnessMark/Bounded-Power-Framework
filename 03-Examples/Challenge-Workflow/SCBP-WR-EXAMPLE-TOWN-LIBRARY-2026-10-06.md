- Name, and place: [TOWN] Public Library, main branch, [TOWN], [STATE].
- Which power, in plain words: blocking web pages on the public computers and Wi-Fi (internet filtering).
- Registration record, if one exists: SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-TOWN-LIBRARY, power P3. This is the library's own authorization record; the library has adopted the Core Requirements.

## 2. What happened

2a. **Summary.**

In the witness's own words (unchanged):

> "I use the library computers most days because I don't have internet at home. Since last week a lot of pages about local news and community groups are blocked. The block page says the category is 'Social and Forums'. I thought the filter was only supposed to block the kinds of pictures the subsidy law covers. I asked at the desk and the librarian said the vendor's update turned some new categories on and they'd look into it. Nothing has changed yet."

2b. **Timeline.** *(Put in order by the interviewer from the witness's answers; the witness checked it.)*
- Around 2026-10-06: the witness first saw pages blocked under a category called "Social and Forums".
- 2026-10-08: the witness asked at the reference desk. The staff member said a vendor software update had switched on new categories and that staff would look into it.
- 2026-10-21: pages in that category were still blocked when the witness filed this report.

2c. **Impact.** The witness could not reach local news comment pages, a neighbourhood association forum, and a support group's message board from the library. The witness has no home internet, so there was no other way to reach them that week.

2d. **Who is affected.** Anyone using the public terminals or Wi-Fi, especially people without home internet and patrons under 17, who cannot switch the filter off themselves. Number affected: UNKNOWN.

2e. **Earlier attempts to resolve it.** Asked at the reference desk on 2026-10-08 (see timeline). No change seen by 2026-10-12. The witness did not use the block page's unblock request, because adults can switch the filter off for their own session; the witness says the problem is that the category is switched on at all.

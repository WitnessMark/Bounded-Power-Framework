## 1. Statement

Around October 6, 2026, at the public computers in the main reading room of the [TOWN] Public Library, main branch, [TOWN], [STATE], I, reading-room-regular, saw that a lot of pages about local news and community groups were blocked. The block page said the category was "Social and Forums".

I have a library card. I use the library computers most days because I don't have internet at home. I thought the filter was only supposed to block the kinds of pictures the subsidy law covers.

**Observations, in order.**

- **Around 2026-10-06.** I first saw pages blocked under a category called "Social and Forums".
- **2026-10-08.** In person, at the reference desk. I asked about it. The staff member said a vendor software update had switched on new categories and that staff would look into it.
- **2026-10-12.** I saw no change.
- **2026-11-05.** Pages in that category were still blocked when I filed this report.

From the library, I could not reach local news comment pages, a neighbourhood association forum, or a support group's message board. I have no home internet, so I had no other way to reach them that week.

Anyone using the public terminals or Wi-Fi is affected, especially people without home internet and patrons under 17, who cannot switch the filter off themselves. I do not know how many people are affected.

I did not use the block page's unblock request. Adults can switch the filter off for their own session. The problem is that the category is switched on at all.

I made a note of the dates in a notebook.

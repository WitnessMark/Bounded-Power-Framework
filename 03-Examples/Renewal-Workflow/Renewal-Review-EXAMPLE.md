## What renewal is

Renewal is a **new authorization decision** made on current evidence. It is never automatic and never presumed (Definitions: Renewal). Continued necessity is a reason to ask for renewal, not permission to continue (3.E.2). **Renewal cannot approve growth.** Growth needs a new authorization. **Incomplete evidence means no renewal.**

The board must record a finding on **every** requirement of 3.E.2 and 3.E.3. A renewal without those findings is void, and the power is treated as expired (3.A.2).

## 1. Evidence the library submitted (3.E.2)

| Required evidence | What was submitted | Board's finding |
|---|---|---|
| **Original grant vs today, in all six dimensions** (3.B.1) | Scope: one growth event. A vendor update switched on two unauthorized categories; a witness report of type `challenge` led to their removal (see the [Challenge Workflow](../Challenge-Workflow/Challenge-Workflow-README.md)). Duration, reach, speed, intensity, opacity: unchanged [EX] | Met. The growth was found and corrected before renewal. Nothing grown is being approved |
| **Harm record against thresholds** | T3-1 wrongful blocks: highest 90-day rate 1.6% against a limit of 2% [EX]. T3-2 slow unblocking: never above 3% against 5% [EX]. T3-3 identity: no record found linking a person to a block [EX]. T3-4 unequal blocking: one review, completed in 70 days [EX], found a list error and corrected it | Met. No threshold crossed. T3-1 came close; see conditions |
| **Fresh tests of every stop path** | Stop signal: test switched filtering off everywhere in 25 minutes [EX]. Automatic stop: tested on the test system. People's path: threshold of 30 people [EX] confirmed as published | Met. All three paths work; holders still independent |
| **Slow-accumulation indicators** (3.A.6) | Blocks per session: flat [EX]. Share of young patrons' sessions with a block: flat [EX]. **Requests per block: down by a third [EX]** | Concern. See section 2 |
| **Drift review by independent testers** | A reviewer from another county examined reinterpretation, accumulation, stop friction, capture, lock-in, and vendor dependence. Found the vendor-update path as the main drift risk [EX] | Met, with a condition (section 4) |

## 2. Warning signs (7.7)

Any of these requires an **expanded** independent review before renewal is decided.

| Warning sign | Present? |
|---|---|
| Stop signals falling while activity grows | **Partly.** Sessions rose about 10% [EX] while unblock requests per block fell [EX] |
| Approvals getting faster without more evidence | No |
| Fewer external auditors taking part | No |
| Interpretation concentrated in fewer hands | No |
| More people affected who never chose to be | No |

Because one sign was present, the board ordered an expanded review. It interviewed young patrons and staff. It found that the weekly staff review of blocks now unblocks most wrong blocks before anyone asks, so fewer people need to ask. That explains much of the fall. Two patrons said they had stopped asking because the request felt embarrassing [EX]. The board treated that as a burden on self-advocacy (2.C.2), not as noise.

## 3. Necessity and reduction (3.E.3)

- **Why the power is still asked for.** A national law still ties the library's internet subsidy to filtering of certain image categories. That is an external, independently checkable condition [EX]. The library's own usage figures were not relied on, because they have not been independently audited (3.E.3).
- **Is it shrinking over time?** A power that stays static fails renewal unless independently verifiable conditions justify it. A reduction in a dimension the power never used does not count. The library proposed two real reductions in dimensions P3 does use:
  - **Persistence:** keep block logs 14 days instead of 30 [EX], with the reviewer drawing its sample weekly instead of monthly.
  - **Intensity:** a young patron's unblock request that staff have not decided within 15 minutes is unblocked automatically [EX], instead of waiting.
- **Finding:** necessity shown by an external condition; reduction shown in two used dimensions. Met.

## 4. Decision

**Renew narrower.**

- **Term:** 3 years [EX], from the end of the current term. Within the 5-year limit of 7.2 for a power that is no longer new and does not use force or confinement.
- **Narrowed:** the two reductions in section 3 become conditions of the authorization.
- **New condition on the vendor:** the vendor's category settings are locked. The independent reviewer checks them every month against the authorized list. Any difference is treated as growth and switched off at once (3.B.1, 4.5).
- **Unchanged:** thresholds T3-1 to T3-4, the stop paths, the ending plan, and "no new categories without re-entry". A threshold can be tightened at any time, but loosened only through re-entry (3.A.5).
- **Recorded findings:** the board's public record states its finding on each item of 3.E.2 and 3.E.3 (sections 1–3 above) and what the people it heard said. Without these findings the renewal would be void (3.A.2).
- **Next renewal:** this board may decide one more renewal of P3. The one after that goes to a different independent body named in advance (3.A.2).

## 5. What happens to the record

The library prepared its own authorization record, so the library issues **version 2** of it. Version 2 has a section headed "What changed from version 1" that quotes, word for word, every passage it changes: the term, the two new conditions, and the vendor-settings check. Version 1 stays public. Nothing is silently replaced.

## 6. What would have meant no renewal

- Evidence missing on any item in section 1.
- Growth found and **not** corrected. Drift found and not corrected bars renewal (3.E.2).
- A stop path that no longer worked or whose holder was no longer independent.
- Persistent growth in the slow-accumulation indicators without correction (3.A.6).
- Necessity resting only on "it has always existed" or on the library's own unaudited data (3.E.3).

If the board had refused, P3 would end at the end of its term. Nothing would continue it (3.E.1, 3.E.6). Filtering would be switched off, the vendor contract ended, and logs deleted with a deletion record, as the ending plan says. Losing the subsidy is an accepted cost of stopping (3.C.4).

## Teaching notes (non-binding)

- Renewal is a fresh decision on current evidence, by a body independent of the power (3.A.2, 3.E.2).
- Corrected drift is evidence a power can be kept within limits. Uncorrected drift bars renewal.
- A falling number of complaints is not automatically good news. Find out why (7.7, 2.C.2).
- Necessity must come from outside the power, and the power must shrink in dimensions it actually uses (3.E.3).
- A renewal changes the authorization record only through a new version that quotes its changes.

# Response — [CITY] Water Department — to SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-CITY-WATER-UTILITY v1

## 1. Summary

The department responds on two points, both about P2 (shutoff of water service for non-payment).

1. **Correction (medical protection).** The record describes the department's medical protection as a letter that "delays shutoff for 21 days [EX], renewable once". That understates the published rule. The department's Customer Service Rules contain a medical-certificate protection that bars shutoff, and requires restoration, for as long as a clinician keeps certifying the medical need, within a yearly cap. The department asks the preparer to correct the B5 row and the B4 Stop paths cell that rest on the narrower description.
2. **Disagreement (whole-power stop path).** The record states that nothing can stop P2 as a whole. The department disagrees. It points to two paths it believes stop all shutoffs: a City Council resolution suspending shutoffs, and a state moratorium on shutoffs ordered during a past public-health emergency.

The department does not dispute any other part of the record in this response.

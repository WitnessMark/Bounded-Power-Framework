# Examples

This folder shows the framework at work on **kinds** of institutions. Every example is fictional. None is a record of any real institution, place, or person.

- Names are placeholders in brackets: `[CITY] Police Department`, `[COUNTY] Jail`, `[STATE] Board of Nursing`.
- Illustrative figures are tagged `[EX]`. They show realistic sizes, not any real body's numbers.
- National laws are named only in general terms ("the federal fair credit reporting law").
- Every file carries the banner **WORKED EXAMPLE — not a record of any real institution.**

v1 of the framework publishes worked examples, not records of real institutions. If you make a record of a real institution with the [AI Registration Bundle](../00-Reading/AI-Registration-Bundle.md), that record is yours. Publishing it is your choice and your responsibility.

All examples use the v1 forms and **Core Requirements v1**. Each record's front matter states the form and vocabulary version it follows. Every record and report here passes the checker (`../02-Forms/validate_record.py`); the classification test case and the README files are not records. Rule numbers like 2.D.3 or 3.C.5 refer to the [Core Requirements](../01-Framework/SCBP-04-Core-Requirements.md).

Each registration example records an institution and then each of its **powers** separately (P1, P2…). For each power it compares what is **observed** with what the Core Requirements **require**, and gives a finding. Each ends with short **teaching notes**.

Two examples are **authorization records**: the institution has adopted the Core Requirements and records its own limits (Town Library, Mutual-Aid Network). The rest are **observed records**: an outsider's description of an institution that has not adopted them.

## The 32 worked examples

### Force, confinement, and removal

| Example | Powers recorded | Main teaching point |
|---|---|---|
| [City Police Department](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-CITY-PD.md) | Use of force; stops, arrests and short holding; plate readers and cameras; tow, impound and forfeiture | One institution, four powers. Nothing can stop force or arrest as a whole; a consent decree reaches one practice (3.A.1, 3.A.7) |
| [County Jail](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-COUNTY-JAIL.md) | Pretrial custody; force by staff; phone, commissary and pay-to-stay fees | Pretrial custody must meet all of 2.D.3(b); repeated Floor violations are a pattern (3.D.5) |
| [National Immigration Enforcement](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-NATL-IMMIG-ENF.md) | Civil detention; removal from the country; bought data and biometrics | Calling detention "civil" changes nothing (1.3, 2.D.3); removal is irreversible (3.D.2); bought data is collected data |
| [Regional Health System](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-REGIONAL-HEALTH-SYSTEM.md) | Involuntary psychiatric hold; restraint, seclusion and medication without consent; medical-debt collection | A private nonprofit can hold force and confinement powers (1.3, 2.D.1–2.D.3) |
| [School District](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-SCHOOL-DISTRICT.md) | Suspension and expulsion; restraint and seclusion; student-device monitoring | When everyone affected is a child who must attend, exit is not a check (2.C.3); a board readopting its own code is not authorization (3.A.2) |
| [State Child Protective Services](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-STATE-CPS.md) | Emergency removal; central abuse registry; hotline screening tool | When a temporary change of legal status counts as containment (3.D.2) |
| [County Family Court](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-COUNTY-FAMILY-COURT.md) | Custody and parenting-time orders; contempt jail for unpaid support | Support owed is not escalation, but added penalties are (2.B.2); civil contempt jail fits no ground in 2.D.3 |
| [County Health Department](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-COUNTY-HEALTH-DEPT.md) | Isolation and quarantine; food embargo and recall; emergency declaration | Containment is stopping harm (3.D.2); emergency powers expire in 90 days (1.6) |
| [Fire and EMS District](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-FIRE-EMS-DISTRICT.md) | Fire suppression; emergency medical response; ambulance billing | Protective operations (3.C.5); billing is not protective |

## The version-2 record and the authority responses

A record never changes silently. An institution answers with an **authority response**, filed beside the record. Only the person who made the record can issue a new version, and the new version quotes everything it changed.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Water utility response](SCBP-RSP-EXAMPLE-CITY-WATER-UTILITY-2026-10-21.md) | A **correction** (the medical protection was stronger than recorded) and a **disagreement** (whether the city council counts as a whole-power stop path) |
| [Water utility, version 2](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-CITY-WATER-UTILITY-v2.md) | The preparer accepts the correction, declines the disagreement, makes two clarifications of its own, and quotes every change. Version 1 stays public |
| [County jail response](SCBP-RSP-EXAMPLE-COUNTY-JAIL-2026-10-21.md) | A **change notice** announcing institutional improvements, including a death-in-custody review board and force thresholds. The county has not adopted the Core Requirements, and the notice does not establish compliance |
| [Data broker response](SCBP-RSP-EXAMPLE-DATA-BROKER-2026-10-21.md) | A **disagreement** with a finding. It stays beside the record; readers judge |

## The bad-faith example

[SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-BAD-FAITH-CONSULTING.md](SCBP-REG-EXAMPLE-BAD-FAITH-CONSULTING.md) is an authorization record a firm files about itself, written to look compliant while avoiding the rules. ## What you will see again and again

- **NONE EXISTS** only where the example establishes that a safeguard is absent. Where it does not, the answer is **UNKNOWN** and the finding is *compliance not demonstrated*. Absence is never inferred because it is common for a kind of institution. A finding is not an insult.
- **Nothing can stop the power as a whole** in many examples. Courts, appeals, and oversight bodies usually reach one case or one practice. These partial safeguards are real and are recorded for what they do. A legislature or other body counts as a whole-power stop path only if it is independent of the institution and has an existing, usable procedure that can stop the power within a defined time, such as a sunset vote that must take place or a standing review body with power to end it. The general ability to pass a new law, or to abolish the institution, does not count: where that is the only route, the answer is NONE EXISTS and the finding is not conforming. A vote by the institution's own governing body never counts.
- **Self-help is not a stop path.** Paying, complying, leaving, or switching a setting off does not stop a power.
- **Findings are diagnostic** for institutions that have not adopted the Core Requirements. They describe the gap; they compel nothing (Core Requirements, "Force and status").

## Gaps

The set does not yet cover everything. Known gaps: eminent domain (covered only by analogy in the Tax Collector), confinement as a criminal sentence, a public university, and a tenant union or similar member-organizing body.

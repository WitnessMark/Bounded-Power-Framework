# Witness Report Examples

A **witness report** puts on the record what an institution did, failed to do, or threatened to do. One form covers five kinds of report: `incident`, `stop_signal`, `challenge`, `change_request`, and `review_petition`. The form is the [Witness Report Form 1.0.0](../../02-Forms/02-Witness-Report.md).

Every example is fictional:

- It carries the banner **WORKED EXAMPLE — not a record of any real institution.**
- People appear by role, never by name.
- Places and institutions are placeholders in brackets.

## Making your own

- **With an AI:** the [AI Witness Interview Bundle](../../00-Reading/10-AI-Witness-Interview-Bundle.md) turns an AI chat into a calm interviewer that builds the report with you.
- **By hand:** follow the [Witness Report Filing Guide](../../00-Reading/09-Citizen-Witness-Report-Filing-Guide.md).

Your report is yours to keep. There is no place to submit witness reports in v1 yet; the filing guide explains how to share one safely if you choose to.

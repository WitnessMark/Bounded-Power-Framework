# Start Here

Welcome. This page helps you find the shortest path to what you want to do.

If you want to know why the project exists, begin with the [repository README](../README.md).

> **v1 is a draft for independent review.** No institution or government has adopted it. It contains worked examples of fictional institutions and BPF's own record, and no records of real institutions.

---

## What are you here to do?

### Something happened to you

Something an agency, company or other institution did, or is still doing, has affected you.

> **If anyone is in danger right now, contact emergency services first.**

1. **Write it down.** Read the [Witness Report Filing Guide](09-Citizen-Witness-Report-Filing-Guide.md) (five minutes). It explains how to make a dated, honest record of what happened, safely.
2. **Or let an AI interview you.** The [AI Witness Interview Bundle](10-AI-Witness-Interview-Bundle.md) turns an AI chat into a calm interviewer that asks one question at a time and builds the report with you.
3. **See what you can do next.** [What You Can Do](07-What-You-Can-Do.md) (US-focused) covers complaints, records requests, oversight offices, and courts, with their deadlines.

**The report is yours.** v1 has no place to file reports; nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.

---

### See the framework applied

The **worked examples** are the main way to see how the framework works in practice.

Recommended path:

1. Open [03-Examples](../03-Examples/).
2. Pick an institution you know something about: a police department, a county jail, a school district, a water utility, a data broker, an owners' association, a town library, and more.
3. Read how the example lists the institution's powers, what can stop each power, and how each compares with the Core Requirements.
4. Read the **teaching notes** at the end of each example.

All examples are fictional. Names, places, and figures are illustrative. Some examples also show an institution's authority response, and one shows a record updated to a second version.

### Make your own record of an institution

You want to record an institution that affects you.

Recommended path:

1. Read a few worked examples first, so you know what a good record looks like.
2. Read the step-by-step [Citizen Inquiry Guide](05-Citizen-Inquiry-Guide.md), then download the **AI bundle** from [02-Forms](../02-Forms/).
3. Open an AI assistant and attach the bundle.
4. Name the institution and where it is.
5. Choose which of its powers to record.
6. Read the draft. **UNKNOWN** means the AI could not find something; **NONE EXISTS** means it found a safeguard missing.
7. Check the sources yourself, and run the checker if you can.

**The record is yours.** It is not sent anywhere and not filed with this project. If you publish it, that is your choice and your responsibility. See [TERMS](../TERMS.md).

You can also fill in the registration form by hand. The forms are in [02-Forms](../02-Forms/).

## A note on scope

The Bounded Power Framework is a way to observe, record, and evaluate authority.

It is not a government, regulator, court, enforcement body, or certification authority. It gives no legal advice.

Its purpose is to make authority more visible, understandable, and open to review.

# Purpose and Scope

*Background, not rules. The binding text is the [Core Requirements (SCBP-04)](../../01-Framework/SCBP-04-Core-Requirements.md). Where this page seems to differ from it, the Core Requirements govern.*

---

## Why the project exists

Many large harms do not come from one bad person. They come from authorities that operate without enough structural limits: powers that outgrow their purpose, outlast their reason, spread into new areas, or keep causing harm after the warning signs are clear.

The project asks one question:

**What structural conditions let authority cause harm, and what limits would reduce that harm?**

It does not start from a political ideology, party, or form of government. Power exists in many forms, and every form can fail. The concern is not who holds power. It is how power behaves.

---

## How the parts fit together

Each part rests on the one before, so any rule can be traced back to what motivates it.

1. **Historical data.** Datasets of widely reported failures of authority, mostly in the United States, examined for recurring patterns. They are in [`06-Supporting-Data/`](../06-Supporting-Data/) and are read for the reader in [Historical Context](../../00-Reading/03-Historical-Context.md).
2. **Reasoning.** The argument that power should be treated as a structural problem rather than a moral one: [BPF Reasoning](../../00-Reading/01-BPF-Reasoning.md).
3. **Rules.** The binding rules: the [Core Requirements (SCBP-04)](../../01-Framework/SCBP-04-Core-Requirements.md). v1 has 101 rules in seven parts.
4. **Forms and tools.** Forms that ask the same questions of every authority, power by power: the [registration form, witness report, and authority response](../../02-Forms/), with a shared vocabulary, a checker, and an AI bundle.
5. **Guidance.** The other framework documents explain and apply the rules: the Orientation (SCBP-01), Foundational Theory (SCBP-02), Operations Manual (SCBP-03), Testing Protocol (SCBP-05), Expressing Findings (SCBP-06), Integration and Adversarial Testing (SCBP-07), Witness Reports and Stop Signals (SCBP-08), and Record Quality Standards (SCBP-09).
6. **Worked examples.** More than thirty fictional records show the forms applied to different kinds of institution: [03-Examples](../../03-Examples/).

A later edition of the rules may not take effect until an independent check confirms that it weakens no protection, except by a deliberate change stated with its reasons (6.6). Every superseded version stays publicly archived (6.5).

## Records of real institutions

v1 holds only the fictional worked examples and BPF's own record ([04-Registry](../../04-Registry/)). It contains no records of real institutions.

Why:

- **v1 is published for independent review.** The rules and forms should be tested before they are used to describe real institutions in this project's name.
- **The project cannot check every record.** A record of a real institution can be wrong. A checker can confirm that a record is well-formed; it cannot confirm that it is true ([Validator Coverage Matrix](../Validator-Coverage-Matrix.md)). Publishing such records here would lend them weight they had not earned.
- **Records belong to the people who make them.** Anyone can record a real institution with the forms or the AI bundle. Publishing that record is their choice and their responsibility ([TERMS](../../TERMS.md), section 5).

Records list each power separately; every fact is a sourced value, UNKNOWN, or NONE EXISTS; and a record changes only through a new version that quotes what changed. v1 has no place to submit records or witness reports.

## Purpose

The framework gives a fixed method for examining authority. It asks:

- What powers does an authority hold?
- Who is affected by each power?
- What harm could each power cause?
- What does the authority depend on?
- What can stop each power, and who holds that stop?
- When does each power end?

Its aim is to make authority visible, understandable, and open to review.

---

## Scope

The framework can be applied to any body that holds power over others: governments and agencies, companies, platforms, professional bodies, unions, schools, health systems, nonprofits, member associations, and more. It is not tied to any country, ideology, or institution.

---

## What the framework is not

It is not a government, a regulator, or a court. It grants no authority and removes none. It creates no legal obligations and decides no question of guilt or legality. Nothing in it has been adopted by any institution or government.

The project applies the same questions to itself. [BPF's own record](../../04-Registry/SCBP-REG-0001-BPF.md) finds that most of what the project does is influence only, but that it cannot yet show whether its AI bundle shapes how institutions are described widely enough to count as authority. It says plainly that this is a self-assessment, not a self-certification.

---

## Guiding principle

**Prevent harm where possible. Repair harm where necessary.**

# Supporting Data

Historical datasets behind the Bounded Power Framework. They show the kinds of failure the framework aims to prevent.

**These are history, not records.** Nothing here is a record under the framework, a finding about any institution or person, or a rule. No institution named here has been assessed against the Core Requirements.

New to the project? Don't start here. [Historical Context](../../00-Reading/03-Historical-Context.md) reads this data for a first-time reader and then points back to these files.

## Datasets

- `Major-US-Authority-Harms-1900-2026.csv`: 50 widely reported cases, each cited to one or two public sources (see Sources below), of an authority imposing a cost on people who had not consented, each with a short structural note.
- `Major-World-Structural-Harms-1883-2021.csv`: 12 well-documented cases from around the world, each an authority decision that caused lasting harm.

## Limits

- **Mostly US, and selective.** The cases were chosen as widely reported examples, not as a complete or balanced sample.
- **Compressed.** Outcomes are summarized in a line or two. Some cases may still be under appeal or in litigation.
- **Assembled with AI assistance** from widely reported public events. The US list's rows were later checked against the sources they cite, but not independently verified beyond them. Check any figure against an original source before relying on it.
- **Not updated.** These files are a fixed snapshot, not kept current.

## Sources

- `Major-World-Structural-Harms-1883-2021.csv` has a `sources` column.
- `Major-US-Authority-Harms-1900-2026.csv` has a `Sources` column. Every row cites one or two public sources (mostly government or court documents, official archives, Britannica, or major news organizations), in the form "Title (Publisher) <URL>", with two sources separated by " ; ". Descriptions were checked against those sources and compressed; before quoting a figure, read the cited original. Recent cases are described in neutral terms, and some may still be in litigation.

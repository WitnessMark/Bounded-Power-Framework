# Human Review Summary

*Template version 1.0.0 · 2026-10-13 · For records made with Registration form 1.0.0, Authority Response 1.0.0, and the Core Requirements v1 · Draft, not adopted*

Use this to write down what a person checked in a record, and what they found. It covers the checks the automated checker cannot make: [SCBP-09](../../01-Framework/SCBP-09-Record-Quality-Standards.md), Section IX, and the "Person" column of the [Validator Coverage Matrix](../Validator-Coverage-Matrix.md).

**What a review is not.** It is not a certification, an approval, or an acceptance into any registry. v1 has none. A review does not change the record. Only the record's original preparer can change it, by issuing a new version that quotes what changed. The institution's own answer goes in an authority response, not here.

The review follows the same rules as a record: every claim about a source says where in the source; people in the record are referred to by role, not name; and anything you could not check is marked UNKNOWN, never guessed.

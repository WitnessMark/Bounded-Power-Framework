# Writing Style

*Maintainer resource. The voice in which the framework's reading material is written, and how to keep it.*

The reading material is written in the voice of [BPF Reasoning](../../00-Reading/3-Understand-the-Framework/BPF-Reasoning.md). The aim is writing that seems simple and obvious once it has been read, and that treats the reader with dignity: as an intelligent adult, often one under strain, never as someone to be managed or reassured.

The style applies to every document a person reads: Start Here, the guides, the framework documents, the examples, the README. It does not apply to a witness's own words, which are kept exactly as given, or to the instructions inside the AI bundles addressed to the AI, which are written to be followed, not read.

## The voice

1. **Rules and definitions are stated flatly.** "Duration is capped by default." "A witness report is a dated, written account."
2. **Claims about how the world behaves are stated as tendencies.** "Power tends to expand when it is left unattended," not "power expands". One counterexample defeats an overstatement; a tendency survives it. "May" is too weak for this purpose: it suggests the thing might not happen at all.
3. **Explanation is written in the third person.** Steps a reader carries out are written as plain instructions: "Attach the file." "You" is kept for titles and for the rare place where nothing else reads naturally.
4. **Worked examples, not scenes.** A concrete case drawn from a real kind of power is encouraged: a school district's power to suspend, followed through review and renewal, makes an abstract rule easy to grasp. What is avoided is the wrapper that talks down ("picture a kitchen", "think about a school", "that's the basic idea") and any example built on a confusion readers do not have. The case is stated directly, as a case.
5. **No reassurance or encouragement.** "Gaps are fine" and "that worry is reasonable" become statements of fact: "A report with honest gaps is more useful than one with none."
6. **A likely misreading is closed off at once.** "This is not an ethical preference. It is a containment measure."
7. **Short sentences, one idea each, in plain words.**
8. **Defined terms stay fixed.** The [Glossary](../../Glossary.md) governs. A defined term is never replaced by a synonym for variety.
9. **Structure carries the weight, not adjectives.** "No expiry date exists" says more than any adjective could. Loaded words are left out.
10. **Each thing is said once,** where it is needed. Elsewhere it is linked, not repeated.

## Formatting

- **Bold is for navigation only:** a safety line, a key term where it is first defined, the names of options, the labels a reader scans for. It is never used for tone or emphasis.
- *Italics* mark the occasional line worth noticing, such as a principle or a statement of ownership, and are used sparingly.
- Headings name what follows. Numbered lists are for steps in order; bullets for parallel items; tables for comparison.
- Links within the repository are relative. A file meant to be downloaded and used on its own, such as an AI bundle, uses full web addresses.
- Text format (encoding, line endings, quotes) follows the [Canonical Text Standard](Canonical-Text-Standard.md).

## Before and after

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "If an authority is doing something that harms you, you have more options than most people realize." | "The usual options when an authority causes harm." |
| "Left unattended, their powers widen." | "Left unattended, their powers tend to widen." |
| "Think about a public school. Teachers teach, the cafeteria serves lunch…" | "A school district holds several separate powers over students: to suspend them, to search their belongings under set conditions, to impose discipline." |
| "Picture a restaurant kitchen." | The three roles stated directly, with one real instance: a health inspector can close a restaurant without running it. |
| "The easy way: let an AI interview you." | "With an AI interviewer." |
| "Don't upload photos to the AI chat." | "Photos, video and recordings stay out of the chat." |
| "Your report is yours." | "*The report belongs to its author.*" |

## Using this guide with an AI assistant

An AI assistant can draft or revise in this voice when given two files: this guide and [BPF Reasoning](../../00-Reading/3-Understand-the-Framework/BPF-Reasoning.md), as the model of the voice. The instruction is to rewrite the text in that voice without changing its meaning, its facts or its rule numbers.

Every change of fact or rule in the result is checked against the original before it is accepted. A rewrite changes how something is said, never what is required.

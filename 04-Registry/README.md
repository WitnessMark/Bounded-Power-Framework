# Registry

In v1 this folder holds one record: **BPF's own record**, [SCBP-REG-0001-BPF](SCBP-REG-0001-BPF.md).

## BPF's own record

The project records itself with the same registration form everyone else uses. It lists the project's powers one by one: publishing the Core Requirements, examples and background material; amending the Core Requirements; the checker and the two AI bundles; a witness-report intake that does not operate; designating the official version (the hash registry); and interpreting the Core Requirements. For each power it says what exists and what does not.

It is a **self-assessment, not a self-certification.** The project classified its own powers, and the Core Requirements say no authority may settle its own scope by itself. The record ends with a list of what an independent reviewer should check.

## The public register (planned)

The framework is designed to hold records of real institutions as a public register, and to report across them. A later version is intended to keep registration records as a public record, under published operating rules, and to publish aggregate reports drawn from them: which powers have no independent stop, which have no expiry, which reach a Floor, how findings change from one version of a record to the next, and how many witness reports concern each power and place.

The forms already carry what a register needs: stable record and report identifiers; a link from each witness report to the record and power it concerns (question 2i), and from each power to the reports about it (B11); versions that never replace what they supersede; and authority responses filed beside the records they answer.

How witness reports would be received, and what of them would be published, is for the register's operating rules to set. The Core Requirements already set the standard such a record would follow: it never identifies the people who give reports (7.5).

*v1 does not operate a register.* Nothing is collected, no host has been chosen, and no date is set. A register is intended to open only after the rules and forms have been reviewed, and only once its operating rules are published: how records are checked, how disputes are handled, and how authority responses are published.

## Why there are no records of real institutions yet

v1 is published for independent review. Nothing in it has been adopted by anyone. Until the rules and forms have been reviewed, the project does not publish records about real institutions, and it does not accept them. There is no place in v1 to submit a record or a witness report.

## Where to see the framework applied

The [worked examples](../03-Examples/) show complete records for more than thirty kinds of institution: police, jails, courts, utilities, schools, data brokers, platforms, cooperatives, and more. They are fictional. Some include an institution's answer to a record, and one shows a record updated to a second version.

## Keeping your own records

You can record a real institution yourself, with the [registration form](../02-Forms/01-Registration.md) or the [AI bundle](../00-Reading/2-Examine-an-Institution/AI-Registration-Bundle.md). Run the [checker](../02-Forms/validate_record.py) on your file.

- **The record is yours.** It is not sent to, filed with, or reviewed by this project.
- **Publishing it is your choice and your responsibility.** See [TERMS](../TERMS.md), section 5.
- **Follow the forms' rules:** public sources and your own observation only; people by role, not name; facts kept separate from allegations; UNKNOWN or NONE EXISTS instead of a guess.
- **No silent replacement.** Change a record only by issuing a new version that quotes what changed. If the institution answers with an authority response, publish it beside your record.
- **Numbering.** Keep `NNNN` in the record ID (for example `SCBP-REG-NNNN-SUFFIX`). Only BPF's own record carries a number.

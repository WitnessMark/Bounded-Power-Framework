## 3. Not legal advice, and not an official finding

The Bounded Power Framework is an analytical framework, not an authority. Nothing in this repository is:

- a determination that any institution has broken any law or rule.

The framework does not take away any right you have under law. Rule 2.1 of the Core Requirements says the Floors are a minimum: where a constitution, law, or treaty gives you more protection, that protection still applies. For anything serious, talk to a lawyer or a legal-aid office. See `00-Reading/02-What-This-Is-and-Is-Not.md`.

## 4. Worked examples are fictional

The records in `03-Examples/` are **worked examples**. They describe fictional institutions. Names, places, and figures are illustrative and are not drawn from any real institution. Any resemblance to a real institution is unintended.

v1 contains no records of real institutions. The only record of a real body in v1 is BPF's own record, in `04-Registry/`.

## 5. Records you make with the AI bundle are your own

You can use the AI bundle in `02-Forms/` with an AI assistant to draft a record of a real institution. If you do:

- **The record is yours.** It is not sent to, filed with, reviewed by, or published by this project.
- **Publishing it is your choice and your responsibility.** If you publish it, you are its publisher.
- **Check it before you rely on it.** An AI can be wrong. The checker confirms that each number has a source tag; it cannot confirm that the source says what the record claims. Read the sources yourself.
- **Follow the forms' own rules:** public sources and your own observation only; people referred to by role, not name; proven or admitted facts kept separate from allegations; and every unknown written as UNKNOWN or NONE EXISTS, never guessed.

The same applies to witness reports and authority responses you write with the forms. v1 offers no place to submit records or reports to this project.

## 6. Corrections and the no-silent-replacement rule

**No silent replacement.** A record, or a document of this framework, is never quietly changed or swapped out.

- **Records.** A record changes only by a new version from its original preparer. The new version quotes, word for word, everything it changed or removed, with the reason. Earlier versions stay available.
- **Institutions answer beside the record.** An institution that thinks a record about it is wrong files an **authority response** (`02-Forms/`). The response is published beside the record and never replaces it. Readers see both. Anyone who publishes a record is asked to publish any authority response beside it.
- **Framework documents.** Changes to the Core Requirements and other framework documents are published as new versions, and superseded versions stay public (Core Requirements 6.5). A change that weakens a protection must be stated separately with its reasons (6.6).

**Reporting an error in this repository.** The maintainer's public contact is the GitHub account **WitnessMark**; there is no project email address. GitHub Issues are temporarily closed. Comments open when Issues reopen, through the review-comment form described in the README.

When you report an error, please name the file and the statement, and say what you think is wrong. Corrections, where warranted, are made by a new version with a note of the change.
